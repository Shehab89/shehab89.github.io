About Me

Data in service of people.

I'm a Senior Data Analyst whose work sits at the intersection of technology, humanitarian response, and social change. My career is driven by one conviction: that rigorous analysis can and should be a force for good in the world's most fragile contexts.

After completing my MSc in Applied Data Science at Utrecht University, I embedded myself in the NGO and media development sector — building data pipelines in conflict zones with Internews, modeling political violence trends at ACLED, and managing beneficiary data for thousands of displaced people in Yemen through RECO.

The most important datasets are the ones that remain underfunded, under-analyzed, and overlooked. That's where I choose to work.
